# H30 original STEP correction in 4.1.1

`H30 Enclosed` revision 1.2 removes the hand-built enclosure reconstruction.
Its SCAD and STL exterior representations are now derived directly from the
unmodified official `WHEELTEC_H30 Sensor (metal housing).stp` assembly.

The vendor STEP is used byte-for-byte as the conversion source with SHA-256:

`54f4665afd6962b4bbeb4bd7445c316438f05d08454fff7590fdc6ad6ae3aa8d`

No extra screw instances are added to the geometry. The physical quantities of
four cover screws and four vibration-isolating mounting screws remain catalog
BOM metadata because the official assembly contains only one of each.

The original STEP is not a canonical STEP representation yet because changing
its internal product names to include Narys component identifiers would modify
the vendor file. Its official URL and hash remain the immutable provenance for
the exterior exports.
