# Publish two distinct XL4015 variants

Catalog release: `4.2.0`

The package now exposes two independent public identities:

- `electronics/power-converters/xl4015:xl4015-step-down-5a-cc-cv`
- `electronics/power-converters/xl4015:xl4015-1`

The first retains its original detailed STEP/F3D sources and its existing
optimized 51 × 26 mm screw-terminal SCAD/STL exterior.

The second is a separate 55 × 25 × 15 mm solder-pad layout reconstructed from
the public renderings at <https://grabcad.com/library/xl4015-1> and the linked
UNIT Electronics product dimensions. It has its own self-contained SCAD and
generated STL. Because GrabCAD requires authentication to download
`XL4015_MODELO_PISTAS.step`, the reconstruction is explicitly an exterior
placement/clearance model rather than an exact derivative of that STEP file.
