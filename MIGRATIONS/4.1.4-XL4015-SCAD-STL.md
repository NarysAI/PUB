# XL4015 SCAD/STL migration

Catalog release: `4.1.4`

Stable object identity remains:

`electronics/power-converters/xl4015:xl4015-step-down-5a-cc-cv`

## Preserved sources

- The 6,061,279-byte STEP file remains byte-for-byte unchanged in the package.
- The original Fusion 360 archive remains unchanged under `sources/`.
- The source URL is recorded as <https://grabcad.com/library/xl4015-1>.
- License status remains `unverified`.

## New delivery bundle

- `xl4015-step-down-5a-cc-cv.scad`: self-contained editable exterior model.
- `xl4015-step-down-5a-cc-cv.stl`: generated from the SCAD model and selected as
  the primary catalog representation.

Regeneration command, run from the package directory:

```text
openscad -o xl4015-step-down-5a-cc-cv.stl xl4015-step-down-5a-cc-cv.scad
```

The optimized exterior retains the 51 × 26 mm PCB envelope, four corner mounting
holes, two auxiliary holes, terminal blocks, electrolytic capacitors, adjustment
trimmers, toroidal inductor, regulator package, diode, control ICs, LEDs, and
representative SMD clearance volumes. It deliberately omits internal and cosmetic
detail that does not affect placement or enclosure clearance.

## Validation

- Original tessellated STEP scene: 124,840 faces.
- Generated STL: 3,696 triangles (97.0% fewer).
- Generated STL bounds: 51.0 × 26.0 × 17.2 mm, including terminal pins below
  the PCB; the board itself remains 51.0 × 26.0 × 1.6 mm.
- SCAD and STL were rendered and visually compared before release.
