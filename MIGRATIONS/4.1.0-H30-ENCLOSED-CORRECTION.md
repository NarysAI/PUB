# H30 Enclosed correction in 4.1.0

The exact `h30-enclosed` variant is revision 1.1. Its exterior model now places
the USB-C opening at the center of the front long wall and represents the main
housing and sloped cover as separate physical parts. No cover fasteners are
visible on the top surface.

The physical BOM is:

- 1 x `//pub/electronics/modules/wheeltec-h30:h30-pcb`
- 1 x `//pub/electronics/modules/wheeltec-h30:h30-main-housing`
- 1 x `//pub/electronics/modules/wheeltec-h30:h30-cover`
- 4 x `//pub/electronics/modules/wheeltec-h30:h30-cover-screw`
- 4 x `//pub/electronics/modules/wheeltec-h30:h30-plastic-damper-screw`

The quantities intentionally correct the official STEP assembly, which contains
only one instance of each screw even though the physical product uses four.
