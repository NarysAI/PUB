# 5.0.0 rocker-switch identity replacement

The two earlier rocker packages did not represent the supplied physical sample
and are removed in this breaking catalog release:

- `//pub/electromechanics/switches/kcd1-rocker:kcd1-on-off-on`
- `//pub/electromechanics/switches/kcd1-203-dpdt-rocker:kcd1-203-on-off-on`

Both identities incorrectly associated the sample with the larger 19 x 13 mm
KCD1 family. They are replaced by the ruler-scaled generic identity:

- `//pub/electromechanics/switches/mini-rocker:mini-ii-o-i`

The replacement preserves the observed three-terminal `II-O-I` construction but
uses the photographed 15.0 x 10.5 mm front envelope, 13.8 x 9.2 mm cutout class
and 5.0 mm terminal pitch. No compatibility aliases are retained
because the old geometries were materially incorrect.
